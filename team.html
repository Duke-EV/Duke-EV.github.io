How we work

A complete engineering company, run by undergrads

Aerodynamicists run CFD and validate in wind tunnels. The electronics team designs custom motor controllers and battery management systems from the PCB up. The mechanical team lays up carbon fiber monocoques by hand. Operations handles sponsorships, logistics, and competition strategy.

Members, including first-years, don't watch. They own.
