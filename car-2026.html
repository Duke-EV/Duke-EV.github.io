Why Urban Concept

A harder problem

Prototype-class cars are rolling physics experiments: the driver lies down, the body is a teardrop, and every rule bends toward efficiency.

Urban Concept differs significantly. The rules demand a street-realistic vehicle: upright seating, four wheels, doors, a windshield with a wiper, headlights and turn signals, a horn, a luggage compartment, hydraulic disc brakes. It has to be practical, safe, and legal-looking, and then it has to be efficient.

This is the class that maps to the future of everyday electric transportation.
