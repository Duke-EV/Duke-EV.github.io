Who we are

Student designed and built

Duke Electric Vehicles (DEV) is a student-led engineering team at Duke University dedicated to designing and building ultra-efficient electric vehicles. Every carbon-fiber layup, every custom motor controller, and every manufactured component is done by students.

Record vehicles

World Record Efficiency

We set two world records in efficiency, both certified by Guinness World Records.

Guinness World Record · 2019

Eta: Most Efficient Electric Vehicle

797 mi/kWh. That's 27,482 MPGe, over 175× the efficiency of a Tesla Model 3. Eta could circle the planet on the energy in a single gallon of gasoline.

Guinness World Record · 2018

Maxwell: Most Fuel-Efficient Vehicle

14,573 MPG on hydrogen. Maxwell travels 8.5 miles on less than a gram of hydrogen, dethroning a record that stood for over a decade.
